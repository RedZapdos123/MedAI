FAQ:
Q: What does it mean to be prediabetic?
A: Prediabetes means your blood sugar levels are higher than normal, but not high enough to be diagnosed with diabetes. It's a warning sign that you're at risk of developing diabetes, but it can often be reversed with lifestyle changes.

Q: Why is my cholesterol high, and what can I do about it?
A: High cholesterol can be due to diet, genetics, and other factors. The doctor recommends lifestyle changes like a healthier diet and exercise. If those don't work, medication might be needed.

Q: What kind of exercise is best for me?
