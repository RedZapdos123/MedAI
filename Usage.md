# Usage Guide:

## Introduction:

This guide explains how to use the MedAI application's two main features: MediGen AI and CareChat.

## MediGen AI: Medical Report Summarization:

MediGen AI helps you understand complex medical reports by providing a simple summary.

1.  **Navigate to MediGen AI**:
    From the home page, click on the "Get Started" button under the "MediGen AI" section.

2.  **Upload or Paste Your Report**:
    *   **Upload a PDF**: Click the "Upload PDF" button and select a medical report file from your device.
    *   **Paste Text**: Alternatively, you can paste the text of your report directly into the text area provided.

3.  **Review the Extracted Text**:
    After you upload or paste your report, the application will show you a preview of the extracted text. This ensures that your report has been processed correctly.

4.  **Summarize the Report**:
    Click the "Summarize Report" button to begin the summarization process. The application will send the text to the AI for analysis.

5.  **View the Summary**:
    Once the summary is complete, it will be displayed on the screen. The summary includes:
    *   A plain-language overview of the report.
    *   A list of key findings.
    *   Actionable recommendations.
    *   A set of frequently asked questions (FAQ) to help you understand the report better.

6.  **Download the Summary**:
    You can download the summary as a text file by clicking the "Download Summary" button.

7.  **Start Over**:
    To summarize another report, click the "Upload another report" link.

## CareChat: AI Wellness Assistant:

CareChat is an AI-powered chatbot for general health and wellness information.

1.  **Navigate to CareChat**:
    From the home page, click on the "Start Chat" button under the "CareChat" section.

2.  **Choose a Chat Mode**:
    You can select a persona for the chat to focus the conversation on a specific area:
    *   **General**: For general health inquiries.
    *   **Lifestyle**: For tips on diet, exercise, and healthy habits.
    *   **Mental Wellness**: For support with stress, anxiety, and well-being.

3.  **Start Chatting**:
    Type your message into the input box at the bottom of the chat window and press Enter or click the send button.

4.  **Review the Conversation**:
    The AI's response will appear in the chat window. You can continue the conversation by sending more messages.

5.  **Crisis Support**:
    If you send a message that indicates a potential crisis, the chatbot will display a banner with links to emergency resources.

6.  **Clear the Conversation**:
    To start a new conversation, you can click the "Clear conversation" button.

## Safety Notice:

Please remember that MedAI is for informational purposes only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult with a qualified healthcare provider for any medical concerns.
